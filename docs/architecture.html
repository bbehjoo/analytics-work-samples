How It's Built — from raw to report in five layers, every layer tested

The whole platform is one dbt project. Raw tables land, staging cleans them, intermediate holds the business logic, marts hold one grain each, and the serving layer is what people and AI agents query. This page is generated from dbt's own model graph, so it always matches the code.

Revenue is a star schema

People data stays behind one door

dim_employee and the headcount fact are private to People Analytics · everyone else reads a secure view or a suppressed aggregate, and the revenue domain joins in through a bridge that carries keys only
