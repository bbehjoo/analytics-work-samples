1 · ARR per employee — hiring vs revenue

Source: rpt_people__revenue_per_employee · company ARR ÷ headcount at each month-end

What it is

The one number that joins the revenue and people domains. It peaked at {{rpe_peak}} in {{rpe_peak_month}} and slipped to {{rpe_end}}.

Why it matters

In the six months after the peak the company added {{h2_hires}} people while ARR grew {{h2_arr}}. Efficiency fell back to where it was a year earlier.

What to do

Use ARR per employee as a guardrail on the next hiring plan: approve headcount against planned ARR so the ratio holds at $200k or better, reviewed monthly next to the ARR bridge.

2 · Attrition — Customer Success is the hotspot

Source: rpt_people__department_summary · Legal has fewer than 5 people, so it shows no numbers
Voluntary attrition, last 12 months

Why it matters

Customer Success loses {{cs_attrition}} of its people a year, every exit voluntary, against {{vol_attrition}} company-wide. It owns renewals, and churn tripled to {{churn_2025}} in the same year. The two may be linked; this data can't show it yet. Pay isn't the obvious cause: the team is paid at its band midpoint.

What to do

Run stay interviews in Customer Success this quarter, and add the missing join (which CSM owns which account) so the next review can test whether accounts that lost their CSM churned more.
