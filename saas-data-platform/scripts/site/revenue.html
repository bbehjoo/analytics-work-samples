Why it matters

Expansion is now the main growth lever, and this is the list that feeds it: accounts already using more than they bought. The {{under_pct}} using less than half is the churn risk ahead of renewal.

What it is

ACV at deal-line grain by close date. Unlike ARR, it counts one-time services and renewals.

Why it matters

Protecting the renewal base matters as much as new sales. Mid-Market is the volume engine, and {{pipeline}} of open pipeline is the forward signal against the new-logo slowdown.

What to do

Track pipeline coverage against the new-logo gap, and watch renewal bookings as the early signal for churn.

4 · Finance hygiene

Dollars at risk by discrepancy · number of records

What it is

Every Salesforce contract matched to its NetSuite order and invoices, with each disagreement put in a bucket.

Why it matters

Contracted revenue with no ERP order is a close-the-books and revenue-recognition risk, not a rounding error.

What to do

Work the {{missing_n}} missing and {{orphan_n}} orphan records first. Auto-clear currency rounding under a materiality threshold. Make this a standing month-end check.
